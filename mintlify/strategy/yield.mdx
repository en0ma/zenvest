---
title: "Yield methodology"
description: "How trading profits and losses are generated."
---

The underlying automated strategy trades Bitcoin-linked 15-minute event markets.

- An event position can settle profitably and produce a positive gross result.
- Fees, slippage and other eligible transaction costs reduce profit.
- Positions can settle at a loss. Net realized returns can be negative.
- Investor participation is governed by the pre-trade allocation snapshot.

Zenvest does not offer a fixed interest rate or promise guaranteed yields. Gross bot results, net settled returns and credited investor P&L are different figures.
