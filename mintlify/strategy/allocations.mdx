---
title: "Allocation and cutoff rules"
description: "How an investor becomes eligible for a trade's outcome."
---

1. The platform records eligible capital assignments with effective timestamps.
2. A trade snapshot freezes participating balances at the relevant cutoff.
3. Settled net P&L is distributed based on eligible allocated capital only.
4. New deposits and unallocated balances do not receive historical trade returns.

Fees, FX conversion, custody and settlement reconciliation must be recorded independently.
