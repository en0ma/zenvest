---
title: "Deposits and withdrawals"
description: "Funding and settlement considerations."
---

Deposits and withdrawals are subject to availability, verification, custody reconciliation, applicable terms and any unsettled trade exposure.

Funds reported as releasable by the trading bot are not automatically approved for investor withdrawal. They must be reconciled against the authoritative account ledger and actual custody balances.

Never send funds using an unsolicited social-media message.
