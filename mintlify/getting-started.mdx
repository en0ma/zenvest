---
title: "Getting started"
description: "Create and secure your Zenvest account."
---

1. Visit [Zenvest registration](https://zenvest-rust.vercel.app/register).
2. Enter your name, email and a unique password.
3. Open your activation email and confirm your address.
4. Sign in to your account.

<Info>Account availability and investment access depend on applicable terms, eligibility and operational status.</Info>

If you lose access to your password, use [Forgot password](https://zenvest-rust.vercel.app/forgot-password).
