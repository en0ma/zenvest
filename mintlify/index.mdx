## The capital lifecycle

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">Register with your display name, email address, and password.</Step>
  <Step title="Review capital and eligibility">See account allocation states and understand when capital becomes eligible for a trade.</Step>
  <Step title="Understand active trading">The independent strategy trades BTC 15-minute event markets. Results can be positive or negative.</Step>
  <Step title="Review settlement and reporting">Reconciled results and eligible capital are used to determine each participating investor's share.</Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>There is no guaranteed yield, liquidity, or preservation of principal. Trading in short-duration Bitcoin event markets is speculative. Review the [risk disclosures](/strategy/risk).</Warning>
