description: "Follow the capital lifecycle from allocation through settlement."
---

## Capital allocation

A capital balance may be held unallocated or assigned to an eligible trading window. **Unallocated capital does not participate in the outcome of trades to which it was not assigned.**

## Trade snapshots

The system uses a cutoff and frozen allocation snapshot to determine participant eligibility. New allocations cannot retroactively participate in an earlier trade.

## Trade settlement

The trading system reports market outcomes and P&L. Investor attribution requires independent custody, fees and settlement reconciliation, and is not inferred from bot-reported liquidity alone.

## Withdrawals

Available capital, open trade exposures and settled funds must be reconciled before withdrawals can be authorized.
